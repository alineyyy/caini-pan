I am a Ph.D. candidate at [NeuroSpin, CEA Paris-Saclay](https://joliot.cea.fr/drf/joliot/en/Pages/research_entities/NeuroSpin.aspx), in the [MIND team](https://team.inria.fr/mind/) (Models and Inference for Neuroimaging Data, Inria & CEA). My thesis develops **high-resolution 4D acquisition strategies for 11.7 T fMRI**: designing spatio-temporal sampling schemes that capture fast brain dynamics, and validating them through simulation and in vivo experiments.

Before my Ph.D., I worked on deep learning for medical image analysis, including computational pathology and multimodal knowledge distillation.

## Research interests

I am interested in machine learning for complex scientific and biomedical data: learning useful representations from high-dimensional images and signals, multimodal and foundation models, and AI agents that reason over scientific tools and knowledge. I am particularly drawn to methods that generalise beyond a single domain.

## Projects & open source

- **[HearSay](https://github.com/alineyyy/Hearsay)** — A five-stage LLM agent pipeline (Strands Agents, Claude on Amazon Bedrock) that splits community advice into checkable claims and rules on each against ~5,500 official French government documents, with the source and its last-update date. Works across languages.
- **[MRI-NUFFT](https://github.com/mind-inria/mri-nufft)** — Python library for non-Cartesian MRI with GPU/CPU NUFFT backends. I contributed differentiable NUFFT operators for learning k-space trajectories.
- **[SNAKE-fMRI](https://github.com/mind-inria/snake-fmri)** — Simulator from neuro-activation to k-space data for benchmarking fMRI acquisition and reconstruction. I contribute to it and lead its in vivo validation.
