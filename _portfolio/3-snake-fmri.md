[Code on GitHub](https://github.com/mind-inria/snake-fmri)

SNAKE simulates fMRI from neurovascular coupling to k-space acquisition, so that sampling and reconstruction methods can be compared against a known ground truth. I contribute to the package and lead its in vivo validation on 3D stack-of-spirals acquisitions.
